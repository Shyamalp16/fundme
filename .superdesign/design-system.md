# fun(d)ME Superdesign Context

Design a responsive pre-launch marketing website for fun(d)ME at fun.me. The future product lets an adult make one public ask, then lets each stranger give exactly $1 to it once, with ten supported asks allowed per person each week.

This V1 is an advertisement and waitlist—not a product demo. Do not add example asks, fake creators, a browse feed, an ask-creation form, support/pledge buttons, fundraising totals, testimonials, countdowns, or invented activity. No real money moves here.

## Primary job

In three seconds communicate: “Make one public ask. Let enough strangers give it one dollar each.”

Single conversion CTA: **Join the waitlist**. A secondary “See how it works” anchor may support comprehension.

## Art direction

Use a sophisticated high-contrast, typography-first editorial system inspired by Swiss structure, but warm and internet-native. The concept is “The Power of One”: singular units become meaningful through repetition.

- Paper `#F3F0E8`; light paper `#FFFDF7`
- Ink `#171714`; secondary ink `#5D5B54`; hairline `#CBC6B9`
- Signal coral `#FF5C45`; pressed coral `#C73525`; support mint `#BDE3D3`
- Clash Display 600–700; Satoshi 400–700
- 12-column asymmetric desktop grid
- Hairline editorial rules
- Pills only for actions and waitlist controls
- One abstract equation object may use an 18px radius and hard mint offset shadow

The hero headline is the single focal anchor. Its secondary visual is an abstract `$1` equation labeled “Not live yet,” never a fictional ask. A quiet outlined “ONE” may sit behind the hero because it communicates the concept.

## Required structure

1. Sticky navigation: exact `fun(d)ME` wordmark, domain signature, The idea, How it works, The rules, Join the waitlist.
2. Hero: “What if the internet gave you $1—one stranger at a time?” with early-access CTA and abstract equation “one person × enough strangers = something worth doing.”
3. Premise: “A dollar is tiny. A crowd isn’t.” Explain the one-dollar and ten-weekly-choice mechanic.
4. How it works in future tense: Make one clear ask; put it in front of people; let the crowd decide.
5. Conceptual viral loop: Make the ask → Share the link → Get a $1 yes → Reach someone new.
6. Rules: one dollar per person/ask, ten choices weekly, no rewards/investments/raffles/receipts, nothing live yet.
7. Waitlist: email, intended role, explicit consent, honest success state.
8. Footer: contact, anchors, “Pre-launch · No real money accepted.”

## Interaction and layout

- Maximum width 1440px; gutters `clamp(1rem, 4vw, 4.5rem)`; section rhythm `clamp(5rem, 10vw, 9rem)`
- Mobile is one column with 16px gutters and 44px targets
- Use spacing and alignment before containers
- Coral primary action, ink text, subtle -2px hover
- Visible keyboard focus; accessible menu; waitlist errors and confirmation announced
- Reduced motion disables transforms and smooth scrolling

## Hard vetoes

No purple/blue gradients, glassmorphism, floating coins or blobs, fake dashboards, equal feature-card grids, arbitrary serif italics, fake social proof, multiple conversion CTAs, glow stacks, charity framing, or copy that implies payments are active. The result should feel modern and high-craft, neither meme-heavy nor generic SaaS slop.
